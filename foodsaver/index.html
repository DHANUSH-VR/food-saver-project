The Problem

Why We Built This

At marriages, events & offices — people invite X guests but only Y attend. X always > Y. Surplus food goes to waste while millions go hungry.

X > Y
Guests invited vs attending — food always surplus
~33%
Of all food produced never reaches a human mouth
💰
Cost savings when food is redistributed efficiently
🌿
Reduced carbon footprint from food in landfills
😩

Existing System is Broken

Donors had to personally visit organizations, call multiple NGOs — time-consuming and exhausting.

🚀

Our Solution

A seamless platform connecting donors, admin, and delivery agents — food collected and delivered within hours.

🤝

Community Outcomes

Decreases hunger rate. Food will not get wasted. NGOs, charities and needy individuals benefit directly.

📱

Mobile Friendly

Works on all devices — donate food from your phone in under 2 minutes, from anywhere.
