❓ Help & FAQs
How to donate food? +
Register on the platform, login as a user, fill the food donation form with food name, type, quantity, and your address. Our team will collect the food from your doorstep for free.
How will my donation be used? +
Your donated food will be collected by our delivery team and distributed to registered NGOs, charities, and directly to needy individuals in your area.
What food can I donate? +
You can donate Raw Food (uncooked grains, vegetables), Cooked Food (prepared meals), and Packed Food (sealed packaged items). All food must be fresh and safe to eat.
What should I do if my food is near expiry? +
Please only donate food that is fresh and safe to eat. Do not donate expired or spoiled food. If you're unsure, contact us before donating at [email protected].
Is this service free? +
Yes! Food Saver is 100% free for donors, NGOs, and delivery volunteers. Our mission is to reduce food waste and fight hunger — no charges involved.
